## Decisions made today
- Login-only content: only users who follow the source and proved access with their own login (valid 30 days) may read it; admins always.
- Fresh login per scan with stored credentials; never retry a rejected login; a pause until a person rescans.
- Scan saved in one transaction; scans over 20 MB are refused with a reason.
- Retry: 3 tries, budget 10 per scan, abort once with one reason when the site is down.
- Scheduler: 06:00 Korea time, catch-up at startup, one worker per site, skip overlapping, retry once after an hour.
- Downloads: any version, 60-second links, logged and capped.
- Hospital IDs: keyed hash only; credentials entered through the admin API (owner's choice over a local script).
- Hosting: not Render free (sleeps, 512 MB); one Ubuntu server on AWS EC2 with Docker, Caddy and a Cloudflare DNS record.
